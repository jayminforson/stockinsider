# Brand - StockInsider

_Status: active_

## Product
StockInsider is a technical-premium stock intelligence desk for live market data, explainable forecasts, and focused watchlists.

## Direction
Technical + premium. The visual language is dark graphite, electric mint, warm paper, and signal coral. Keep the experience calm and instrument-like: strong hierarchy, compact data density, and confident editorial moments.

## Palette
- Graphite: `#0d1110`
- Panel: `#151b18`
- Paper: `#f2f0e8`
- Mint signal: `#b8f26a`
- Coral alert: `#ff846d`
- Muted sage: `#87918a`

## Typography
- Display/UI: Space Grotesk
- Numeric/metadata: IBM Plex Mono

## Usage
- Use mint for positive signal and primary actions.
- Use coral only for negative movement or attention states.
- Keep charts high contrast and label important values directly.
- Avoid generic gradients, rounded card stacks, and excessive decoration.
